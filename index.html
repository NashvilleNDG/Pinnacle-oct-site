Our Design

  • In today’s competitive hospitality marketplace, standing out matters – not just with “a look” but with thoughtful and thorough design that strengthens guest satisfaction, that encourages repeat visits, and that builds your business with higher RevPAR and ROI
  • Every hotel has a story to tell whether it’s a business destination, a getaway resort, or a historic property – our incredible designers create incredible spaces that tell your property’s story, that give it personality, and that make it memorable
  • Sometimes we start with prototype design that can range from paper sketches to digital simulation – it’s our way to test ideas, get your feedback, and refine design quickly and inexpensively before proceeding to final development
  • From concept through completion, you get absolutely nothing cookie-cutter – we balance aesthetics, comfort, function, and operations to design a unique environment that offers the comforts of home, the convenience of the office, and the indulgence of a vacation
The result:

Your hotel looks exceptional, feels welcoming, and works beautifully – and when guests leave your hotel, their experience stays with them

Our Procurement

  • Procurement is where great design meets disciplined implementation – we carefully manage hundreds of purchasing decisions and thousands of dollars in FF&E expenditures on your behalf to make sure every purchase supports the design, budget, schedule, and long-term performance of your property
  • We select the right products for your approved design, then identify the best sources
  • Our purchasing power with manufacturers and suppliers gives you an edge as we negotiate details like unit pricing, volume discounts, payment terms, warranties, quality, durability, delivery schedules, and freight costs
The result:

Your products arrive at the right time, in the right sequence, and in the right condition thanks to proactive procurement designed to protect your investment, to reduce surprises, and to keep your project moving

Our Manufacturing

  • We partner with a select network of vetted and proven factories in Asia to assign orders for high-quality case goods based on the facility’s expertise, tariff implications, lead times, and other relevant factors
  • We start with 3-D renderings that show what the finished product will look like in your space, then we generate detailed, machine-readable shop drawings that guide the manufacturing process with precision
  • Our representatives on site supervise pre-production samples (PPS) for perfect fit, function, and finish, then manage quality assurance inspections at multiple steps of factory production.
  • Finally, we focus on dependable delivery of your products – we track your shipment daily with specialized computer programs and keep you fully informed through final arrival at your job site or warehouse
The result:

Unmatched ability to avoid damage, delays, and unexpected costs

Our Installation

  • Hotels wear products out – carpeting, draperies, wallpaper, electronics, and other FF&E must be properly installed so they perform well and withstand wear, so they meet manufacturer requirements and keep warranties valid, and perhaps most importantly, so they protect your guests
  • Our trained, trade-certified crews bring years of on-site hotel experience to your property so they do the right things in the right way
The result:

Our business philosophy is simple and we take it very seriously – if a job is worth doing, it’s worth doing exceptionally

A Founder And CEO
With A Hospitality Mindset

Jason Allen has a proven ability to enhance the look and the value of hotel properties. With more than 30 years of hospitality experience, his approach is straightforward: cutting costs not corners, while exceeding expectations for owners, travelers, and brands.

For owners, he delivers profits and peace of mind without sacrificing quality. For guests, he offers style, comfort, and convenience. For brands, he interprets standards in a fresh way that still maintains a recognizable identity.

Jason knows that no owner goes into business to be “average,” so his style is to see beyond “the obvious” to “the possible” without losing sight of “the practical.”

He earned his undergraduate degree in marketing at Georgia State University and founded Pinnacle South in 2003 in partnership with the late Tommy Howle, a respected hospitality professional.

Now Jason pushes himself and his team to take every project personally – making it different, better, and smarter as if it was their own.
